Senior Product & Visual Designer · Dublin, Ireland

I’m a product designer of complex, data-heavy engineering tools, with 12 years of experience at Intel.

I led the end-to-end UX/UI design of Intel Graphics Performance Analyzers (GPA), a core graphics profiling tool for Intel’s application engineers and graphics engineers at AAA game studios such as Epic Games (Unreal Engine), Rockstar Games, Ubisoft, Blizzard and Capcom.

I co-founded Spark, Intel’s company-wide design system: one design language for GPA, VTune, Advisor, CVAT, Geti and other products.

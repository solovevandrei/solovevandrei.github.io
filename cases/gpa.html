GPA — Graphics Performance Analyzers

UX/UI of four tools: Graphics Monitor, Frame Analyzer, Trace Analyzer, System Analyzer

This page is a placeholder. The full case study is in progress.

GPA (Graphics Performance Analyzers) is Intel’s toolset for optimizing games and graphics on Intel hardware. Intel built it as an investment in its developer ecosystem: to help studios get the most out of Intel graphics.

It is used by graphics engineers at game studios such as Epic Games, Rockstar Games, Ubisoft, Blizzard and Capcom, by independent developers and the open-source community, and inside Intel by application and driver engineers.

Spark — Intel design system

Co-founded Intel’s company-wide design system

This page is a placeholder. The full case study is in progress.

Spark is Intel’s company-wide design system. It gives products from different Intel organizations one design language: GPA, VTune, Advisor, CVAT, Geti and others.

It started as a working group of designers who wanted to free product teams from duplicated design work and endless debates over styling, so they could focus on users’ problems. Its design tokens and coded components became a single source of truth for designers and developers.
